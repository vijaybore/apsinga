CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about rooftop solar, government subsidies, and installation.

What is PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana and who is eligible? +

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana is the Government of India's flagship rooftop solar scheme that provides a direct central subsidy of up to ₹78,000 for residential customers installing 3 kW or above solar systems. Any Indian household with a valid DISCOM (like MSEDCL) connection and their own house/property is eligible. Apsinga handles the entire online application process on the national portal on your behalf.

How much does a typical residential solar system cost in Pune? +

A 3 kW residential on-grid solar system typically costs ₹1.80 – ₹2.20 Lakhs before subsidy. After the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar central subsidy, your effective out-of-pocket cost is approximately ₹1.00 – ₹1.40 Lakhs. With monthly savings of ₹3,000–₹5,000, you typically recover your investment within 3 to 4 years. All prices vary by panel/inverter brand and your specific roof requirements.

How many days does the full solar installation take? +

For a standard residential system (1-5 kW), physical installation takes 1 to 3 days. After installation, the complete MSEDCL net metering application and approval process takes 15–45 days depending on the DISCOM workload. Apsinga manages the entire end-to-end process, and your system starts generating power immediately after commissioning even before net metering approval.

What happens during power cuts (load shedding)? Will solar work? +

Standard On-Grid solar systems automatically shut off during power cuts (as a safety requirement). To continue using solar during outages, you need a Hybrid Solar System with battery storage. Apsinga offers both On-Grid (most economical, for grid-connected areas), Off-Grid, and Hybrid (with Lithium battery backup) configurations depending on your needs and budget.

What maintenance is required for solar panels? +

Solar systems require minimal maintenance. Monthly dry cleaning (or quarterly water wash) of panels is recommended to maintain peak efficiency. Apsinga offers Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) that include 2–4 cleaning visits per year, inverter health checks, thermal scanning, and performance reports. Our 25-year warranty on panels and 5-year warranty on inverters covers any manufacturing defects.

Can I install solar if I have a flat/apartment or live in a housing society? +

Yes! Housing societies and apartment complexes can install communal solar systems on the common rooftop. The generated power is used for common-area electricity (lifts, lights, pumps) significantly reducing maintenance charges. Individual apartments generally cannot install solar unless they have exclusive terrace access. Apsinga has designed and installed several housing society solar systems across Pune.
